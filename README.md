## 📑 Task 1: Robotics in Healthcare
This task explores the role of robotics in modern healthcare.  
Key points covered:
- Applications in surgery, rehabilitation, patient care, diagnostics, and hospital logistics.  
- Benefits such as precision, reduced human error, and improved patient outcomes.  
- Challenges including cost, training, and ethical concerns.  
- Case studies highlighting real-world adoption.  
- Future directions like AI integration, nanorobotics, and collaborative robots.

👉 Refer to the detailed report (`Robotics_in_Healthcare_Report.md`) for the full write‑up.
